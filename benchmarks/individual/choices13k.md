# Choices13k risky choices

[Benchmark index](../README.md) | [Shared protocol](../protocol.md)

**Role:** principal task. **Coverage:** 1,764 inputs per condition.

## Input structure

- `two options`
- `payoffs and their probabilities`
- `source choice metadata`

## Output and measures

Output: A; B; mapped to higher/lower payoff variance when defined.

Measures: choice distribution; percentage-point shifts from Base.

## Construction and scope

Retain 1,766 source choices; 1,764 have unequal payoff variances and enter the reported variance comparison.

Human choice rates are descriptive references, not correct-answer labels. The retained source panel controls known probabilities and lack of feedback.
