# Controlled stock paths

[Benchmark index](../README.md) | [Shared protocol](../protocol.md)

**Role:** principal task. **Coverage:** 1,005 inputs per condition.

## Input structure

- `67 path shapes`
- `five net changes`
- `three volatility levels`
- `fixed final price of 100`

## Output and measures

Output: BUY; SELL; HOLD.

Measures: action distributions across controlled conditions.

## Construction and scope

Cross the path, net-change and volatility factors while holding the final price fixed.

SPACE-authored numerical task. The exact path records are not distributed; the task structure is documented here.
